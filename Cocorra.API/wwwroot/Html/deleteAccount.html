Delete Your Cocorrá Account

Use this page to permanently delete your Cocorrá account and its data without opening the app. Sign in with the email and password you use in the app to confirm it's you.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email, age, profile picture)
  • Your voice verification recording
  • Your messages, friends, friend requests and blocks
  • Rooms you host, room participation, invites, topic requests and votes
  • Your notifications and reports you've filed

What is kept

  • Reports other users filed about you are kept for safety and moderation, with your identity removed.
  • Anonymous usage statistics that can't be linked back to you.
Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. You'll need to register again to use Cocorrá.

Forgot your password? Reset it from the login screen in the app, then come back here. If you can't sign in, email support@cocorraapp.com from your registered address with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and we'll delete your account for you.
